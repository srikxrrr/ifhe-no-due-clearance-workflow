# ROI / Net Time-Saving Calculations

**All values below are hypothetical estimates, not measured IFHE data.**

## Per-request model

| Activity | Current baseline (min) | Proposed (min) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Intake and routine status handling | 12 | 7 |
| Department follow-up / coordination | 8 | 4 |
| Final review and closure | 4 | 2 |
| Additional register review | 0 | 1 |
| Support and exception allowance | 0 | 1 |
| **Total** | **24** | **15** |

Current total = 12 + 8 + 4 = 24 minutes/request.  
Proposed total = 7 + 4 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 15 minutes/request.  
Net saving = 24 − 15 = **9 minutes/request**.  
Relative reduction = 9 ÷ 24 × 100 = **37.5%**.

## Hypothetical monthly scenario

Assumed request volume = 100 requests/month.

- Current: 100 × 24 ÷ 60 = 40 staff-hours/month.
- Proposed: 100 × 15 ÷ 60 = 25 staff-hours/month.
- Net: 40 − 25 = **15 staff-hours/month**.

## How to replace estimates with measurements

For each request, measure active staff minutes separately from elapsed calendar time. Include training, review, support and maintenance effort in the proposed process. Use comparable case types and disclose sample size, measurement period and exceptions. Do not treat synthetic CSV timestamps as actual measured time.
